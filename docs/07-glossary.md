# Glossary

- **Line:** A tracked zebrafish strain or cohort record.
- **Generation:** The recorded generation number of a line.
- **IDed number:** The count of fish identified for the line; separate from freezer vial counts.
- **Protocol:** A method and fields used to identify fish.
- **Cryopreservation record:** A stored sample range, location, and date.
- **Feeder:** A person who can see the line overview and the Feeding Schedule but cannot edit or chat.
- **Guest:** A read-only session opened through a controlled link. It sees the line overview only (a restricted role, like the Feeder), not records, history, exports or chat.
- **Feeding Schedule:** The monthly calendar of who feeds the fish on which day, with the lab's other scheduled events.
- **Assignment:** The person who feeds on one day. A change keeps every earlier value in the day's history.
- **Schedule event:** A non-feeding entry on a day of the Feeding Schedule (for example a water quality test); it can repeat weekly.
